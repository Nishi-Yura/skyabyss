# 深淵の骨の橋・巣の門・塩の湖 (docs/design/36 の 8 章・A9): 橋の上と下、巣の戸、引き潮と満ち潮の湖、高い棚
# ./gradlew runShots -Pscript=tools/shots/abyss_layers_bone_salt.txt -PshotsName=abyss_layers_bone_salt
world abyss_layers_bone_salt flat
size 1280 720
timeout 2400
render 12
clouds off

wait 80
cmd skyabyss cinematic stop
cmd gamemode spectator
hud off
cmd skyabyss abyss enter
wait 40
cmd skyabyss abyss build
wait 900

# 骨の橋: 手前の端から向こうの端へ、それから橋の下から見上げる
cmd tp @s -8 170 -10 -70 8
wait 60
shot b_bridge_start
cmd tp @s 3 168 -8 -70 40
wait 60
shot b_bridge_down
cmd tp @s 6 150 -6 -70 -6
wait 60
shot b_bridge_under

# 巣の戸と肋の番 (戸は閉まる前の姿)
cmd tp @s -18 146 -2 90 4
wait 60
shot b_den_door

# 塩の湖: 引き潮 (時間 0)、満ち潮 (時間 1200)
cmd time set 0
wait 80
cmd tp @s -16 52 12 -131 10
wait 60
shot s_lake_low
cmd tp @s -4 52 -18 -20 20
wait 60
shot s_causeway_low
cmd time set 1200
wait 80
cmd tp @s -16 52 12 -131 10
wait 60
shot s_lake_high
cmd tp @s 6 67 -8 -60 10
wait 60
shot s_shelf_high
quit
